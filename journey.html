My Taekwondo journey

From white belt to 3rd Dan WT Certified Black Belt

A look back at how I got here — and where this is headed next.

Early childhood

First steps on the mat

Started Taekwondo as a young child — first class, first bow, first taste of a sport that would stick for life.

Color belt years

Building the fundamentals

Progressed through the color belt ranks, learning poomsae, sparring basics, and the discipline of consistent practice.

Rising through the ranks

Toward the black belt

Sharpened forms, board breaking, and sparring technique while taking on more responsibility as a senior student.

June 6, 2026

3rd Dan Black Belt

Certified 3rd Dan WT Black Belt by Grand Master Ji Ho Choi at the Ji Ho Choi Taekwondo Institute.

Now

Building for the community

Combining TKD experience with AI and coding skills to build training apps for the community — with a focus on making them accessible to special needs children.
