My Taekwondo journey

From white belt to 3rd Dan WT Certified Black Belt

A look back at how I got here — and where this is headed next.

Early childhood

First steps on the mat

Started Taekwondo as a young child — first class, first bow, first taste of a sport that would stick for life.

September 22, 2018

Blue Belt — 4th Gup

Promoted to Blue Belt, 4th Gup, certified by the Ji Ho Choi Taekwondo Institute Black Belt Center.

2022

Advancing through the color belts

Continued testing through the Green and Yellow Gup ranks at the Black Belt Center, building toward black belt eligibility.

2023

Black Belt achieved

Earned 1st Dan Black Belt at the Ji Ho Choi Taekwondo Institute, capped off by testing up through Blue, 2nd Gup that April.

July 7, 2023

Special Needs Mentor Training

Completed a 3.0-hour Level I training program for mentors and instructors of children with special needs, through the Spectrum Taekwondo Instructor Education and Development program.

August 9, 2023

Certificate of Recognition — Spectrum Taekwondo

Recognized by Spectrum Taekwondo and the Jiho Choi TKD Institute for 10 volunteer hours as a mentor during the PEC (Parents with Exceptional Needs Children) summer program, demonstrating the Five Tenets of Taekwondo throughout.

February 14, 2026

2nd Dan — Kukkiwon Certified

Officially Kukkiwon-certified 2nd Dan Black Belt (Certificate No. 09878182). Kukkiwon certificates are typically issued roughly a year after testing, so this documents a promotion earned earlier.

June 6, 2026

3rd Dan Black Belt

Certified 3rd Dan WT Black Belt by Grand Master Ji Ho Choi at the Ji Ho Choi Taekwondo Institute.

Now

Building for the community

Combining TKD experience with AI and coding skills to build training apps for the community — with a focus on making them accessible to special needs children.
