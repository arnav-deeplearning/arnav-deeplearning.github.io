Coaching tips for every student

Practical, sensory-friendly ideas for instructors and parents adapting Taekwondo training for special needs children — drawn from adapted physical education practice and peer-reviewed research, and tagged by the needs they're most relevant to.

Not a substitute for individualized guidance.

These are general suggestions drawn from adapted physical education practice and published research, meant as a starting point for instructors and parents — not medical, therapeutic, or diagnostic advice. Every child is different; when in doubt, loop in your child's occupational therapist, behavioral specialist, or care team, and always ask the student (or their parent) what actually helps them.
